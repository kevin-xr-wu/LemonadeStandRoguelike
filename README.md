# World Lemonade Stand

A roguelite lemonade stand game. Make as much money as you can in 14 days.

## Play

Open `world-lemonade-stand.html` in a browser. Nothing to install or build.

## How it works

Each day:

1. Check the forecast. Hot days are busy; rain keeps people home.
2. Pick a spot to sell.
3. Buy supplies.
4. Set ice, signs and price.
5. See how you did.
6. Pick an upgrade for tomorrow.

You start with $30, and the money you end with is your score. After day 14, keep your score or keep playing in Endless.
