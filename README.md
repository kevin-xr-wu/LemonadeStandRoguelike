# World Lemonade Stand

A lemonade stand game. Make as much money as you can in up to 100 days.

## Play

Open `world-lemonade-stand.html` in a browser. Nothing to install or build.

## How it works

Each day:

1. Check the forecast. Hot days are busy; rain keeps people home.
2. Pick a spot to sell.
3. Buy supplies.
4. Set signs and price.
5. See how you did.
6. Pick an upgrade for tomorrow.

Play as long as you like, up to day 100. Press End run in the top bar to stop and keep your score.

Your 10 best runs go on the High scores board, with the cash and days of each. The game saves the board in a cookie.

## Core mechanics

- You start with $30. The money you end with is your score. If you can't pay for one cup, the game ends.
- The forecast is always right. Hot days bring more people; rain brings fewer.
- Ice goes in each cup for you. Hotter days use more: none under 70°F, up to 4 cubes at 100°F and up.
- Each flavor has tags: Sweet, Sour, Fancy, Chilled, Fizzy. Each kind of customer likes different tags, and comes by a little more often when you sell a flavor they like.
- Serve at least half the people who come and your rating goes up. A higher rating brings more people.
- Pick an upgrade or a new flavor after each day. Rarer ones show up less often and do more.
