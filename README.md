# World Lemonade Stand

A roguelite lemonade stand game. Make as much money as you can in 14 days.

## Play

Open `world-lemonade-stand.html` in a browser. Nothing to install or build.

## How it works

- Start with $30. The money you end with is your score.
- Check the forecast, buy supplies, and set up your stand each day.
- Match flavors to your customers, keep your stars up, and chain good days for bonuses.
- After day 14, keep your score or keep playing in Endless.
